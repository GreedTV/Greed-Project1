About Sinners

A place to watch streams together, and so much more.

Why Sinners?

Following streamers means jumping between Kick, Twitch and YouTube, each with its own tab, chat and player. Sinners puts them all in one place: watch up to four streams side by side, in HD, with your own volume and quality controls.

Then we made waiting for a stream fun. Browse YouTube, play quick games, level up a pixel adventurer, fight bosses with everyone, and hang out in one chat that follows you across every stream. It's all free, and it all happens on one account: log in with Discord, Twitch or Kick, and connect the others whenever you like.

Questions

Is Sinners free?

Yes, completely. Watching, chatting, the games, the adventure and the Discord bot are all free.

Do I need an account?

Not to watch. Log in with Discord, Twitch or Kick (top right) to chat, earn points, get an adventurer and save your progress. You can connect all three to one account on your Profile. We only see your name and picture.

Are you part of Kick, Twitch or YouTube?

No. Sinners is an independent fan site. The streams and videos are played with each platform's own player and belong to them and the creators.

Are points real money?

No. Points are just for fun: they can't be bought, sold or turned into money. See the Terms of Service.

How do I use the Discord bot?

Type /help in our Discord for a guide with buttons. Your Discord account is your Sinners account, so everything you do there shows up here too.

How do I report something or ask a question?

Message the Sinners team on Discord. Gallery links are reviewed before they appear, and chat is moderated.

Ready to sin a little?

Pick a streamer, grab some points and say hi in chat.
