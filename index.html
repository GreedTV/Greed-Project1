How to use Sinners

A quick guide to each page. Everything works without an account; log in only if you want to chat.

▶

Watch

Open Watch →
  1. Add a streamer: use the Kick, Twitch or YouTube card, type their name (or @handle on YouTube) and press Add.
  2. Wrong person? Click ✎ Fix next to them and paste their channel link instead.
  3. Watch: click a streamer in your list. Turn on Multi-view to watch up to 4 at once.
  4. Controls under the player: volume, quality (Kick and Twitch), ⛶ fullscreen and ⧉ mini player.
  5. Not live yet? Click 🔕 next to a Kick or YouTube streamer to turn on alerts (🔔).
  6. Chat: use the tabs beside the player for Sinners chat or the streamer's own chat. 💬 Hide chat gives the video more room.
⏭

Content

Open Content →
  1. Add videos: paste a YouTube video or playlist link and press Add.
  2. Your queue plays in order and is saved for next time. Click any item to jump to it.
  3. Skip with ⏮ ⏭, or press ⧉ to keep it playing in the mini player.
  4. Quality: YouTube only allows changing it with ⚙ inside the YouTube player.
🎮

Games

Open Games →
  1. Play ⚡ Quick Draw, 🎯 Target Rush or ⌨️ Chat Typer. Logged in, your best score goes on the leaderboard.
  2. Earn points 🪙: you start with 100, and every game you finish earns more (up to 300 a day).
  3. Bet them in 🎨 Color Clash or 🍀 Lucky Number: new rounds every minute, winners split the pot. Points are just for fun.
🖼

Gallery

Open Gallery →
  1. Browse the Images and Clips tabs. Click anything to view it bigger.
  2. Share an image: right-click an image online, choose Copy image address, and paste it.
  3. Share a clip: paste a Kick clip, Twitch clip or YouTube video link.
  4. Shared links appear after a quick review to keep the gallery clean.
⚙

Account & settings

  1. Log in (top right) with Discord to write in Sinners chat. It only shares your name and picture.
  2. Light or dark: switch with ☀️ / 🌙 at the top.
  3. Mini player tip: while a stream is in the mini player, you can open other pages and it keeps playing. Click ⤢ to go back.
  4. Your lists stay on your device: streamers, queue and alerts are saved in this browser.
