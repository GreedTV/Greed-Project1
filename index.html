How to use Sinners

Watching works without an account. Log in with Discord, Twitch or Kick (top right) to chat, earn points, level up an adventurer and join everything below. Connect the others to the same account on your Profile; with Discord connected, our Discord bot uses the same account too.

▶

Watch

Open Watch →
  1. Add a streamer with the Kick, Twitch or YouTube card (name or @handle). Wrong person? ✎ Fix and paste their link.
  2. Multi-view shows up to 4 streams at once. Under the player: volume, quality, ⛶ fullscreen and ⧉ mini player (keeps playing on other pages).
  3. Chat beside the player: the stream's own chat or Sinners chat. Twitch chat is built in: link Twitch on your Profile once and type right here as yourself. Kick chat works the same way: log in with Kick and type with emotes from the 😀 picker. 🔕 → 🔔 on a Kick or YouTube streamer alerts you when they go live.
  4. Watch party: see who else is watching on Sinners and tap 🔥 😂 👏 … to send reactions that float over the video for everyone.
  5. Earn while you watch: logged in, your adventurer gets a little XP every minute (up to 2 hours a day).
📺

Content

Open Content →
  1. Browse YouTube with the search bar or categories like Gaming, 🔴 Live now and Streamer highlights.
  2. Click a video to play it, or + Queue to watch it next. Paste any YouTube video or playlist link too.
  3. Your queue is saved for next time; ⏮ ⏭ to skip, ⧉ for the mini player.
🪙

Points

Your profile →
  1. You start with 100. Earn more from games, daily quests, your login streak, adventures, duels, the world boss and achievements.
  2. Spend them in the Shop: gear, potions, cosmetics and special rewards.
  3. Just for fun: points can't be bought or turned into money.
🎮

Games

Open Games →
  1. Mini games: ⚡ Quick Draw, 🎯 Target Rush, ⌨️ Chat Typer. Better scores earn more (up to 300 a day) and go on the leaderboard.
  2. Betting games: 🎨 Color Clash and 🍀 Lucky Number run a new round every minute for everyone. Winners split the pot plus a small site bonus. The ❓ How this game works box explains it all.
📅

Daily quests & streak

Today's quests →
  1. Every day you visit, claim your 🔥 streak reward: more each day, a bonus every 7th day and an epic item on day 30. Miss a day and it starts over.
  2. 3 daily quests (watch, play, adventure, duel, bet, chat) pay coins and XP. Finish all 3 for a 🎁 bonus chest. New quests at midnight UTC.
🧝

Your adventurer

Open your profile →
  1. Click your name (top right) for your profile. A pixel adventurer is made for you, with ⚔️ Attack, 🛡️ Defense and ❤️ HP.
  2. Level up to grow your stats, and wear gear in 8 slots: helmet, chest, legs, shoes, gloves, weapon, shield, ring. Rarer gear is stronger.
  3. Potions and scrolls boost Attack, Defense, HP, XP or loot for your next 3 fights.
🗺️

Adventures

World map →
  1. Pick a place on the world map: 🌲 Forest, 🕳️ Caves, 🐸 Swamp, 🏔️ Mountains, 😈 Abyss. Each unlocks at a higher level and takes longer.
  2. Your adventurer fights the region's boss while away (you can close the page). Come back and 🎁 Collect: XP, coins and sometimes loot.
  3. The win chance is shown before you go; stronger gear raises it.
🏟️

Arena & world boss

Arena →
  1. Duel other players' adventurers (they don't need to be online). Wins raise your rating; 10 duels a day.
  2. Monthly seasons: the top 3 win coins and a 👑 🥈 🥉 title; ratings then soften back toward 1000.
  3. 👹 Weekly world boss: everyone fights it together. Adventures, duels and ⚔️ Strike (every 4 hours) damage it. When it falls, everyone who fought gets a chest, and the top 3 get gear.
🛒

Shop

Open Shop →
  1. ⚔️ Adventure: gear for all 8 slots in 5 tiers, Wooden to Abyssal, that unlock as you level. Never sells out; sells back for half.
  2. 🧪 Extras: potions and scrolls (3 uses each).
  3. ✨ Cosmetics: hats, capes, pets and chat name colors. Looks only.
  4. 🎁 Redeem: special rewards from the Sinners team, handed out after you buy them.
🏅

Achievements & badges

See yours →
  1. Earn achievements by playing: reach levels, win adventures and duels, keep streaks, beat bosses… Each pays coins once.
  2. Pick one as your badge. It shows next to your name in chat and the arena.
  3. Your Wardrobe on the profile lets you swap hats, capes, pets and name colors any time.
🖼

Gallery & Clip of the Week

  2. 🏆 Vote for one clip a week. On Monday the winner goes on the home page, and whoever shared it gets coins.
