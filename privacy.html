Privacy Policy

What we collect and why. Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information sinnerstv.com ("Sinners", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we keep only what the site needs to work, we don't show ads, we don't use third-party trackers or analytics services, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

When you log in (Discord, Twitch or Kick)

You can log in with Discord, Twitch or Kick, and connect more than one to the same account. From each one we ask only for basic profile access and store your user ID, display name and picture on that site. We don't get your email, password or private messages. A Twitch or Kick login also lets Sinners send the chat messages you type on the Watch page (see below).

What you do on the site

  • Chat: your messages, with your name, avatar and the time.
  • Games: your best scores, your points balance, your bets and their results.
  • Shop: what you bought, when, and whether it was delivered.
  • Gallery: links you share, with the title and name you type (and, if you're logged in, your account, so a Clip of the Week win can pay you), and your weekly clip vote.
  • Twitch and Kick chat (optional): if you log in with or connect Twitch or Kick, we store your ID, name and the access keys they give us (encrypted), and use them only to send the chat messages you type on Sinners. Disconnect any time on your Profile, or remove Sinners in your Twitch or Kick settings.
  • Adventure and rewards: your adventurer, items, quests, streak, achievements, badge and cosmetics.
  • Watch parties: while you watch a stream logged in, your name and avatar are shown to others watching the same stream for a few minutes, and the emoji reactions you send (without your name) for a short time.

Technical information

Anonymous visit counts: to show how many people are online and how many visit each day (on the Stats page), your browser makes a random ID for itself. While a Sinners tab is open it tells our server, about once a minute, that this ID is on a certain page. We don't store your IP address, account or device details with it, so it isn't linked to you. Only daily totals are kept; the per-ID records are deleted after two days.

To stop spam, the number of gallery links shared from your IP address is counted for about an hour, then deleted. Our hosting providers (see below) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

2. What stays in your browser

Some things are saved only in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers: your login session (valid for 30 days), light/dark theme, the streamers on your Watch page, your YouTube queue, live-alert settings and the mini player's position, and the random visitor ID used for the anonymous counts above. Logging out removes the session; clearing your browser's site data removes everything. We don't use cookies for tracking.

3. How we use it

  • to log you in and show your name and avatar next to your messages and scores;
  • to run the chat, gallery, leaderboards, points, betting rounds and shop, and to hand out shop items;
  • to moderate: remove content, ban accounts and prevent spam or cheating.

Your name, avatar, badge, chat messages, leaderboard scores, point totals on the "Most points" list, arena rating, world boss damage, bets in the current round and (while you watch) your watch-party presence are visible to other visitors.

4. Other services

Sinners works with these services, which have their own privacy policies and may receive your IP address or set their own cookies when you use them here:

  • Kick, Twitch and YouTube: their video players and chats are embedded directly from them.
  • Discord: for logging in.
  • Twitch and Kick: if you connect them, for sending your chat messages. Twitch chat is read straight from Twitch; Kick chat is read from Kick through our server. Emote pictures load from Kick and 7TV.
  • Cloudflare: runs our server and stores our data.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the site's fonts and the video library.
  • Images in the gallery load from the sites they were shared from.

5. How long we keep it

Account, game, points and shop records are kept while the site runs, until you ask us to delete them, or until an admin resets them. Chat messages and gallery links stay until they're removed. An admin can remove any of it at any time.

6. Your choices

  • You can use most of Sinners (watching streams, browsing YouTube, playing games without saving scores) without logging in.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us with your Discord name.
  • You can remove Sinners' access in Discord: User Settings → Authorized Apps.

7. Children

Sinners is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for Discord in your country). If you think a child has given us their data, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions, or want your data removed? Message the Sinners team on Discord.

See also our Terms of Service.
